# Design: the instructions rewrite, against humanizer and its forks

Date: 2026-09-24

Dan asked for verve to be reviewed against
[blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) and its most-starred
forks, and for the skill instructions to be rewritten: keep the top-level
patterns and functionality, rewrite how they are written down.

This records what was read, what was taken, what was refused, and what the
evals said before and after.

## What was reviewed

**humanizer 3.0.0** (51.7k stars, 4,139 forks, MIT). Rebuilt on 6 Sep 2026
from 35 patterns to 25 in one 4.6k-word file. Three ideas carry it:

1. **One account of why AI text reads as AI.** A model writes the choice that
   fits the widest range of readers; a person chooses for one. Every pattern is
   a form of that default choice, grouped by the move behind it: staging,
   rhythm by rule, inflation, formatting by rule, leftovers.
2. **Strength ordering.** Patterns are numbered strongest first. The first five
   justify an edit on one sighting; the weak ones are marked *weak alone* and
   need other tells in the same passage.
3. **Currency.** Aligned with the current Wikipedia *Signs of AI writing*:
   false ranges and synonym cycling dropped as human habits or historical,
   vague connection added.

**The forks.** Sorted by stars, most of the top twenty are mirrors or
translations. Seven changed something:

| Fork | Stars | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| jooray/humanizer | 49 | 49 patterns in 10.9k words. New: performed rigour and candour (announced caveat, asserted specificity, the worth-saying certificate), argument residue, reasoning-chain leftovers, forensic residue (zero-width characters, homoglyphs), structural uniformity with a reshuffle test, connective pile-up, hedged-enumeration openers, the treadmill. A detect mode that quotes each tell and refuses a probability. "Count a cluster once." A Slovak and Czech section. |
| jiji262/humanizer-chinese | 25 | 36 Chinese-specific patterns, a register gate before adding any "human flavour", and an over-correction list: fake human is the new AI tell. Invented first-person experience, one fake-casual voice for everything, planted typos, synonym cycling. |
| syq-cmdi/Academic-DeAI | 24 | Academic revision modes, LaTeX safety, protected elements. |
| Matt-Payne/content-humanizer | 7 | A marketing mode: nine patterns switch from delete to make it concrete and keep the sell; SEO structure preserved. |
| vivekkartha/humanizer | 2 | Prestige names in the sentence moved to a references section. |
| kandaiiskandar/humanizer | 7 | A research-writing planning phase, and an AI-percentage detector prompt. |
| ChristopherKahler/humanizer | 4 | A voice-calibration interview that compiles a stored voice profile. |

## Where verve already stood

Ahead of all of them on the things that decide whether a rewrite can be
trusted: seven hard constraints, a two-way fidelity readback, triage that can
decide to do nothing, the politeness check with its warmth half, the overshare
check with its floor, British and American varieties that never reach code or
proper nouns, a preferences file with a closed key list, and an eval harness.
No fork has any of these, and all of them stay unchanged in substance.

Behind in four places:

1. **No account.** The catalogue was humanizer 2.x's flat content, language,
   style, artefacts and filler grouping, with seven groups lettered A to G and
   no reason given for any grouping.
2. **No strength.** A lone curly quote and a not-X-but-Y contrast carried the
   same weight. The strength dial was the only calibration.
3. **Stale and missing tells.** Still listed synonym cycling and false ranges.
   Missing arguing with no one, performed candour, reasoning scaffolding, vague
   connection, repeated openings, uniform structure, connective pile-up, hidden
   characters, and writing about the previous version.
4. **Bloat and history.** A 30-item quick-check list that restated the
   catalogue, and paragraphs narrating past decisions inside the skill.

## What was taken

- **The account**, opening `patterns.md` and `SKILL.md`, extended to verve's
  other two jobs: a person also writes for one reader (group F) and decides
  what to hand over (group G).
- **The families**, as A to E, strongest first. F and G keep their letters and
  numbers, because the evals and the design records cite them.
- **On sight / needs company** on every tell, the strength dial redefined
  against it, and "count a cluster once".
- **New tells**: A5 arguing with no one, A6 performed rigour and candour, B6
  vague connection, C2 repeated openings, C3 uniform structure (with the
  reshuffle test and the treadmill folded in), C4 connective pile-up, E3
  reasoning scaffolding, E4 hidden characters (added to vendor residue), E5
  writing about the previous version.
- **Detect mode**, with jooray's refusal of a percentage. It follows from
  verve's own detector-evasion line: a score is the first step towards tuning
  against one.
- **The over-correction check**, from the Chinese fork, as a step 6 check and a
  section of `voice.md`. The invented narrator is also written into constraint
  3.
- **One-word claims** in constraint 4 (*only*, *first*, *should*, *at the same
  time*): humanizer's fix for its issue #212.
- **The author's sample outranks the dash rule**, as in humanizer: where the
  writer's own writing uses dashes, match its rate.
- **File and embedded modes.** Embedded is how `CLAUDE.md` in `dbhq-uk/dbhq`
  uses verve, before an email reaches Dan; its notes go back to the caller to
  pass on, because they are written for the writer.
- **A "what is not a tell" list**, and "signs of a person" in triage.
- **Sales language in marketing copy** keeps the sell and makes it concrete,
  from the marketing fork, as a line in B4 rather than a mode.

## What was refused

- **An AI-percentage detector** (kandaiiskandar). A number is a guess dressed
  as a measurement, and it is the road to detector evasion.
- **"Qualify universal claims"** (kandaiiskandar's research mode). It changes
  the strength of the writer's claim, which constraint 4 forbids.
- **Pick a side where the source sits on the fence** (the Chinese fork). It
  invents a stance.
- **B5's "cut the unsupported claim"** (humanizer's borrowed authority). Verve
  keeps the attribution and flags it, because deleting *experts believe* turns
  their opinion into the writer's.
- **Voice calibration with a stored profile** (ChristopherKahler). It needs
  setup, state and a slash command; verve is instructions only.
- **Academic and SEO modes** as modes. Too narrow for the cost in length; the
  academic preset and constraint 7 cover the part that matters.
- **Humanizer's default output** of draft, critique and final. Verve returns
  the text and nothing else unless something fired, because it is mostly run
  inside other work.

## Also folded in

Uncommitted work sat on `main` in the shared checkout from 22 Sep 2026: a set
check (a table of opener, paragraph order and closer per piece, fixed by
reordering) and a first-contact length flag. Both are in this rewrite, the set
check as `patterns.md` section S rather than a workflow step of its own. The
first-contact flag has an eval case; the set check does not yet, because a
substring harness cannot compare shapes across pieces.

## Faults the baseline found

A three-run baseline on the old instructions, through the new `--via cli`
path on `claude-opus-5-5`, failed five of the thirteen existing cases in at
least one run against the corpus as it stood, and four once the assertion
below was corrected. The recorded 13/13 was a single run on `claude-opus-5`.
Reading the outputs:

- **F7 contradicted `audience.md`.** F7's example cut *"I completely
  understand how frustrating this must be"* and left nothing; `audience.md`
  said to keep one plain acknowledgement. The model followed the example and
  dropped the apology in three runs out of three. F7 now replaces performed
  empathy with the acknowledgement it stood in for, and says that is an
  apology where the writer is at fault.
- **The cut note paraphrased the cut.** *"That your pipeline is quiet"* in an
  `Overshare (cut):` note. The rule now shows a paraphrase that keeps the words
  as the failure it is.
- **A closer folded rather than cut.** *"Its real win is cutting repeat work"*,
  and *not just X, it's Y* reworded as *more than X. It is Y*. A1 and A2 now
  name both.
- **"Reach out"** was asserted gone by the corpus and absent from the wordlist.
  Added.

Two failures were the harness, not the skill: one call timed out (the runner
now retries a timeout once), and `variety-keeps-american-source` failed a
rewrite that said *behaves* for *behavior*, which Britishises nothing. That
assertion now accepts any form of the word; `must_go` still carries the
variety check.

One case was tightened the other way: `tells-performed-candour` passed on the
baseline with *"so to put it precisely:"*, the certificate reworded. It now
fails any form of *precise*.

## Found on the way

`prompts/install.md` listed seven reference files and fetched seven. The
eighth, `overshare.md`, arrived on 11 Sep 2026 and was never added, so an
install through that prompt produced a skill naming a file it did not have.
Fixed here.

## What the rewrite got wrong on the way

Four revisions, each answering what the previous run showed. Recorded because
three of the faults came from the rewrite itself, and two of those from
importing humanizer's wording without checking it against verve's
constraints.

1. **Triage returned only the line.** *"Return it unchanged with one line"*
   was read as *reply with one line*. Now: the text exactly as given, then the
   line. The old wording had the same fault on Sonnet 5, three runs out of
   three; it was latent, not new.
2. **F5's example taught the hedge.** *I think you need to escalate...* is the
   command with a hedge in front. F5 now recasts it as a recommendation or a
   dependency.
3. **A cluster dissolved mid-edit.** With B8 marked *needs company*, a model
   removed two of three AI words and then judged the third alone. The cluster
   is judged on the source now.
4. **"Drop the significance" dropped claims.** Humanizer's B1 fix, taken
   verbatim, deleted *is evidence of the region's commitment to independent
   data* on Sonnet 5. In humanizer that is the rule; in verve constraint 4
   keeps every claim. B1 and B3 now restate what the dressing claims and cut
   only what names nothing.

Smaller: step 0 now says to carry on where files cannot be read (a model
without tools printed a fake tool call), the stock pleasantry after a greeting
is named in A4, and a letter's closing line keeps its place but not its
jargon.

## Results

Cases that held in all three runs, current corpus, `run.py --via cli`:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `claude-opus-5-5`, all nineteen | 12 | **19** |
| `claude-opus-5-5`, the original thirteen | 9 | **13** |
| `claude-sonnet-5`, all nineteen | 7 | **17** |
| `claude-sonnet-5`, the original thirteen | 6 | **12** |

Six revisions: 16, 16, 19, 17, 19 and 19 on Opus; 16, 13, 15 and 17 on Sonnet
from the third on. The committed runs are from the final text. The last Sonnet
misses are single runs where the model did not follow an instruction the skill
states: commentary in place of the triage text, and a closer folded rather than
cut. More wording would not bind them reliably, so they are recorded rather
than chased.

Every output was read, not only graded. One distortion passed every
assertion: *stands as a testament to the Basque Country's commitment to
independent data* became *was founded in 1989 as evidence of* that commitment
in one Opus run of an earlier revision - the same shape as the 8 Sep finding
in `evals/README.md`.

Two runs were lost to the harness rather than the skill: a batch of calls that
exited 1 with nothing on stderr during a usage limit, and calls that stalled
past five minutes. The runner now retries a stall once and reports stdout when
stderr is empty; both lost runs were rerun whole.

## What this does not settle

**Size.** The rewrite is slightly larger than the old instructions once
inlined (101,715 characters against 98,752, about 4%): `SKILL.md` fell from
3.2k words to 2.8k, and the catalogue grew by the new tells, the false-positive
list and the set check. Whether a much shorter skill would do as well is still the open question
`evals/README.md` asks, and still untested.

**The set check** has no eval case.

## 25 Sep: what a second Wikipedia-based skill added

Dan passed over a `de-ai-writing` skill built on the same Wikipedia guide and
asked what was worth taking. Every candidate was checked against Wikipedia's
raw text (the guide carries an "updated August 2026" banner) rather than taken
from that skill's copy.

**Taken, because the guide has them:** leads that define their own title
(*X refers to...*), stock and paired headings (*Awards and recognition*, *X
and Y*), tables too small to be tables, *has generated debate about* and
*prompted broader reflection on*, *several sources* when one or two are cited,
*such as* before a complete list, the plain-word swaps the guide lists among
signs of human writing (*wrote* for *authored*, *moved* for *relocated*,
*used* for *utilised*, *tried* for *attempted*, *died* for *passed away*), and
the three ChatGPT vocabulary eras (GPT-4, GPT-4o, GPT-5), now a column in
`wordlist.md`. *Widely regarded* and *research has shown* came from
Wikipedia's general weasel-words page, so B5 carries them as weasel words,
not as model tells. *Passed away* keeps an exception: chosen for a grieving
reader, it is courtesy.

**Not taken, because the guide does not have them:** *ventured into ... as*
and *began his career as*, which that skill added.

**Declined by Dan:** a rule to hold length limits (SEO titles, meta
descriptions, button labels), and a rule to edit where copy lives rather than
its built output.

**Refused on verve's rules:** fixes that invent a detail to replace puffery,
advice added for an unknown (*call ahead*), fixing a vague attribution by
making it the writer's own view, flagging *rather than* as a tell, a regex
scanner with a zero-hit target, and "one or two em dashes is fine".
